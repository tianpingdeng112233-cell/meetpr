## Quick reference

- **Brand:** MeetPR — iOS-native powerlifting (squat/bench/deadlift) training app for serious coaches and their students.
- **Visual DNA:** SBD Apparel (pure black + saturated red), industrial/lab equipment, Apple Pro launch language. Reject pastels, gamification, wellness-app aesthetics, emoji.
- **Tokens:** see `colors_and_type.css` — dark-first, single brand red `#E5221E`, SF Pro + SF Mono.
- **Voice:** confident, terse, technical. "Squat W3D1 published. Notify Chen Lei?" — never "Yay! 🎉".
- **Components & screens:** see `ui_kits/ios_app/`.
- **Icons:** SF Symbols in production (Lucide as preview proxy — `assets/icons/MAPPING.md`).
